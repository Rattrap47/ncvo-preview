You matter. You are heard.

We’re a North Carolina nonprofit that gets veterans together to hunt and fish — to talk, to connect, and to have a good time doing it. Every veteran is welcome.

Join the group What we’re about

Why we do this

Too many veterans are lost to suicide. We believe that bringing veterans together — giving them room to talk, and a great experience while they’re at it — can help bring that number down.

Hunt and fish

Time in the woods and on the water with people who’ve been where you’ve been. Outings are posted in our Facebook group.

Be heard

No speeches and no pressure. Just a place to talk — or not — with other veterans who get it.

Stay connected

More than 1,500 members and growing across the state. The group is there between outings, too.

The number we want to lower

17.5 veterans a day died by suicide in 2023, according to the VA’s most recent report.

Source: U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, National Veteran Suicide Prevention Annual Report (2023 data).

One is too many. Being around people who understand is part of how that number comes down. If you’re struggling right now, help is one call or text away.

How to join

  1. Join the Facebook group. It’s public, and it’s where outings are announced first.
  2. Say hello. Introduce yourself if you want to — branch, era, whether you’d rather hunt or fish. Or just read along for a while.
  3. Come out when you’re ready. Details for each outing are posted in the group.

If today is a hard day

You don’t have to wait for an outing. The Veterans Crisis Line is free, confidential and open 24/7.

  • Call 988, then press 1
  • Text 838255
  • Chat veteranscrisisline.net

Support the mission

North Carolina Veterans Outdoors is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Donations, volunteers, landowners with land to hunt or water to fish, and businesses willing to help with an outing all make a difference.

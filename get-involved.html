Get involved

Whether you served or you stand with those who did, there’s a way to help.

Veterans: join us

The Facebook group is the front door. Join, say hello if you want to, and come out to an outing when you’re ready.

Join the group

Volunteer

Outings take people who show up and pitch in. Tell us a little about yourself and what you’re good at, and we’ll be in touch.

Offer to help

Host or partner

Landowners with land to hunt or water to fish, guides, outfitters, charter captains and local businesses: if you can host an outing or help make one happen, we want to hear from you.

Donate

Every gift goes toward getting veterans outside and together.

Other ways to give

  • Access to land to hunt or water to fish

  • Spreading the word to a veteran who needs it

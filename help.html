Get help now

If you or a veteran you know is in crisis, you don’t have to wait, and you don’t have to go through it alone.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

Veterans Crisis Line

Free, confidential support, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — for veterans and the people who love them. You don’t need to be enrolled in VA benefits or health care.

  • Call Dial 988, then press 1
  • Text 838255
  • Chat Chat online
  • TTY 1-800-799-4889 or dial 711, then 988

Worried about someone?

  1. Ask. Ask them directly how they’re really doing. Asking won’t make things worse.
  2. Listen. Let them talk without trying to fix it or judge it.
  3. Stay with them. Help them call, text or chat with the Veterans Crisis Line — you can call on their behalf, too.

More support

  • Vet Center Call Center

    Talk with a fellow veteran about your military experience or readjusting to civilian life. 877-927-8387, 24/7.

  • National Call Center for Homeless Veterans

    For veterans who are homeless or at risk of becoming homeless. 877-424-3838, 24/7.

  • VA mental health care

    mentalhealth.va.gov

  • NC Department of Military and Veterans Affairs

    Benefits, services and veterans resources across North Carolina. milvets.nc.gov

North Carolina Veterans Outdoors is a peer community of veterans, not a crisis or medical service. We’re here for the long haul — and when it’s urgent, the people above are the fastest help there is.
